Why now

Why now

2023–24

First cameras that sign photos ship (Leica, Sony). The industry agrees on a common standard, backed by 6,000+ organizations.

2025

Google Pixel signs photos by default. Proof-of-origin reaches consumer phones — photos only, flagship only.

Aug 2026

EU law now requires AI-generated content to be labeled. Proving what's real is the complement every publisher will need.

Jan 2028

California will require new cameras and phones to embed proof-of-origin by default. The window for independent verification is now.

Video — the hardest and most valuable case — is still unclaimed territory: no phone on the market seals video with a mark that survives social media. TrustCam does.

Transparency

Honest by design

Verdicts that say exactly what we know

Green — untouched, or matched to its verified original. Amber — we know where it comes from, not what happened to it. Red — seal broken, or no proof at all. When we're not sure, we say so — we never call a modified copy authentic. See the verdicts.

What a proof means

“This exact file was recorded by this specific phone, at this time — and is unchanged since.” Nothing is taken on trust: the proof is re-checked from scratch every single time you verify.

What it doesn't

No camera can prove the scene was real — filming a screen produces a genuine recording of a fake. We say precisely what we can prove and nothing more. In this market, overclaiming is fatal; our limits are public.
