The three possible results

✓

Verified

The file is byte-for-byte identical to the registered capture and the hardware signature checks out. Authentic and unmodified — the strongest claim we make.

!

Origin traced

The invisible watermark identifies which capture the pixels derive from, but the copy was modified since — a harmless platform re-encode or a deliberate edit look the same to a watermark. Never proof of authenticity, only of origin.

✕

No proof found

Either the file was never captured with TrustCam, or too little of the original survives — e.g. a crop keeping less than about half of the image, or a screenshot of a small preview. We can't tell these two cases apart, so we claim nothing.
