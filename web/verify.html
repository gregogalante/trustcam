The three possible results

✓

Verified

This is the exact file that came out of the camera, and its seal checks out. Authentic and untouched — the strongest claim we make.

!

Origin traced

The invisible mark tells us which capture this image comes from, but the copy was changed since — social-media compression and a deliberate edit look the same to the mark. Never proof of authenticity, only of origin.

✕

No proof found

Either the file was never captured with TrustCam, or too little of the original is left — say a crop that keeps less than half of the image, or a screenshot of a small preview. We can't tell these two apart, so we claim nothing.
