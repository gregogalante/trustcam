Photo — the sealed original

Captured with the app on a Motorola moto g75 at the sensor's full 4:3 resolution. The proof travels inside the file: invisible watermark, hardware signature, hardware attestation, trusted timestamp and a C2PA manifest.

What the proof carries

Seal
ECDSA P-256 signature from the phone's secure element — attestation chain verified to Google's hardware root, verified boot, official app
Timestamped
existed no later than 2026-08-30T14:09:24Z — RFC 3161 token from a public authority, 4 seconds after the shutter
Credentials
C2PA / Content Credentials manifest embedded — inspect it with any Content Credentials validator (development credentials for now: external validators show the signer as untrusted)
Mark
invisible watermark carrying capture id 82975582… — it survives social-media re-encoding and points any copy back here
